## The Real Problem Real Estate Agencies Face on WhatsApp

Most real estate leads come in outside business hours, on weekends, or during already busy shifts. Brokers take too long to reply, clients lose interest, and they move on to a competitor. This cycle repeats every day across agencies of all sizes.

The solution is not hiring more people to stay online around the clock. It is deploying an AI agent to handle WhatsApp conversations from the first message all the way to a scheduled property visit.

## How the Flow Works in Practice

The process is broken into clear steps:

1. **First contact:** the client sends a message through WhatsApp, whether from an ad, a property listing portal, or the agency website. The AI agent responds within seconds, naturally and warmly.
2. **Lead qualification:** the agent asks strategic questions: property type, preferred location, budget range, payment method, and urgency. All done conversationally, without feeling like a form.
3. **Property suggestions:** based on the answers, the agent filters the portfolio and presents the most relevant options, complete with photos, prices, and descriptions directly in the chat.
4. **Answering questions:** documentation requirements, condo fees, square footage, financing options, and availability. The agent responds accurately without the broker needing to step in for every question.
5. **Visit scheduling:** when the client shows interest, the agent checks available time slots and confirms the appointment automatically, notifying the assigned broker.
6. **CRM logging:** all interactions, lead data, and scheduling status are saved in real time, giving the broker full context before the follow-up conversation.

## What Changes in Day-to-Day Operations

- No lead goes unanswered, regardless of the time of day
- Brokers receive only qualified leads who are ready to talk about visiting a property
- The time between first contact and a scheduled visit drops from hours or days to minutes
- The team focuses on closing deals, not answering repetitive messages
- Automated reports show which channels generate the most qualified leads
